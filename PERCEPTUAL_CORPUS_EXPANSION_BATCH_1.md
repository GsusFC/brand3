- Strong example of infrastructure-first strategy language.
- Useful for category-to-surface translation.
- Useful for claim/signal gap handling because the page makes a strong sovereignty claim without proving adoption.

### CLCripto

- Clean formal TL;DR page with compact block structure.
- Useful for evidence-bound behavior and condensed strategic layout.
- Useful as a direct continuation of the pilot corpus style.

### Meta4

- Early LLM strategy experiment.
- Useful for showing how expressive strategy language can exceed the evidence available.
- Useful for claim/signal gap and evidence-bound reading.
