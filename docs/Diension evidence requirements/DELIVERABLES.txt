SPECIFICATION DETAILS
================================================================================

Each dimension has explicit contract:
  • Evidence goal (what it measures)
  • Epistemology (owned vs. external, comparative vs. standalone)
  • Ready requirements (conditions to score)
  • Thin requirements (has evidence but insufficient)
  • Blocked conditions (insufficient for narrative)
  • Allowed/disallowed evidence types
  • Minimum externality percentage
  • Can score with zero evidence? (yes/no)
  • Requires comparative? (yes/no)
  • Collection must provide (upstream requirements)
  • Evidence Packet validates (hardening rules)

COHERENCIA
  ├─ Ready when: 2+ owned surfaces with consistent message/tone/visual
  ├─ Epistemology: Intra-entity alignment (owned-only sufficient)
  └─ Critical rule: Visual coherence allowed; schema/robots.txt blocked

PRESENCIA
  ├─ Ready when: Owned domain + 2+ external channels, 40% external minimum
  ├─ Epistemology: Multi-channel detectability
  └─ Critical rule: Requires external corroboration (owned web alone insufficient)

PERCEPCIÓN
  ├─ Ready when: 2+ independent external sources with sentiment, 100% external
  ├─ Epistemology: External perception only
  └─ Critical rule: Owned claims forbidden; absence can be meaningful (special case)

DIFERENCIACIÓN
  ├─ Ready when: Direct competitor comparison + external echo, OR 2+ external category-distinctive language, OR competitor corpus
  ├─ Epistemology: Comparative/relational
  ├─ Blocked when: Zero comparative evidence AND zero distinctive external language
  ├─ Critical rule: Owned claims NEVER sufficient; must have external validation
  └─ Most failure-prone dimension (blocks when competitor corpus unavailable)

VITALIDAD
  ├─ Ready when: Last activity ≤60 days + evidence of evolution + consistency
  ├─ Epistemology: Temporal activity signals
  └─ Critical rule: Owned activity sufficient; external press corroborates
